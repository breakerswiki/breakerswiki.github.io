• Button layout

    top: - bottom:

    The Neo Geo MVS originally featured a straight-line button layout. The square layout, introduced with The King of Fighters '94 for better ergonomics, greatly influenced fighting games. Since then, most SNK fighting game titles have adopted this four-button format.

  • Throw

    (Left or Right) +

    Throws beat meaty normals, and some characters have additional throw inputs or air throws. They're also a great way to use the break mechanic, punishing jump attacks and combos with minimal risk.

  • Supers

    The Super gauge holds 3 stocks, each allowing a Super move.

    Super moves deal significant damage and require a special input. You build meter by performing normals, specials, dashes, taunts, and by taking or blocking hits.

  • Tech roll

    On wake-up, ++

    It gives you more options to escape your opponent's pressure after a knockdown. They only work after a non-super hit and can also be delayed.

  • Dashes

    (Left or Right) x2

    All characters except Condor can dash. Some have invincibility frames during backdashes, while others can cancel dashes for quick distance adjustments or longer jumps.

  • Dizzy

    To recover, mash buttons + directions.

    Repeated hits can cause dizziness. While dizzy, mashing directions and buttons skips the effect, triggering immediate recovery with brief invincibility frames.

  • Chip damage

    Chip damage occurs while blocking and can secure a win when the opponent's health is low. Super moves deal minimal chip damage, while special moves are more effective for finishing off an opponent.

  • Break

    You can break specific combos using normals, backdashes, throws, specials, or supers. Focus on mastering unbreakable combos, recognizing breakable ones, and using them strategically to bait reversals.

  • Risk system

    The Risk System increases damage over time if you're inactive. Round winners take more damage, while losers get a small defense boost. The player with lower health is immune to damage boosts until both players health evens out.
