How to play

Controls

Breakers Revenge is commonly played in a square layout A C B D

LP HP
LK HK

Throws

→ / ← +
Some characters have extra throw commands. Throws are also useful for breaking : interrupting certain jumps and combos with low risk.

Supers

The Super Meter holds up to 3 stocks, used for powerful moves with specific inputs. Build meter by attacking, dashing, taunting, or taking and blocking hits.

Combos

The game offers an accessible combo system with numerous cancelable moves. Depending on the character, you can extend combos with juggles through specials or supers.

Back roll

After a non-super hit, press 3 buttons when knocked down to try escaping pressure, resetting the situation, and avoiding follow-up attacks.

Dashes

→→ / ←←
All except Condor can dash. Some gain invincibility on backdash, others can cancel dashes for movement tricks or longer jumps.

Dizzy

Repeated hits can cause dizziness. Mash directions and buttons to skip the stun and recover instantly with brief invincibility.

Chip damage

Chip damage is the health lost while blocking, which prevents indefinite defense and can lead to a KO. While supers do few chip, use specials for finishing an opponent.

Break

Interrupt certain combos with a reversal attack. Master unbreakable combos, spot breakable ones, and use them to bait reversals strategically.

Risk system

Idle boosts damage over time. Round winner receive more damage, losers gain defense. Damage boosts only affect the player with more life until both players are even.

Advanced Tips

A higher Beat-By count significantly impacts both player's attack and defense. In offline tournaments, the game is often reset to ensure fair play.

Anti-airs normals and low-to-the-ground air-to-air can create juggle opportunities, allowing follow-ups into specials or supers.

During a super grab with multi-hits (Bloody Fang or Anubis Hug), players should mash directions and buttons. The attacker can land more hits, while the defender always recover quickly.

Jump attacks hitting a standing opponent and high-hitstun ground moves can be broken. If close, react with a throw, but it can fail if the opponent uses an invincible special or super.
