Beat-By Count

A higher Beat-By count significantly impacts both player's attack and defense. In offline tournaments, the game is often reset to ensure fair play.

Mash during Supers

In multi-hit super grabs (like Bloody Fang or Anubis Hug), both players should mash buttons and directions. The attacker deals more hits, but the defender still escapes quickly.

Breaking with throw

Some combos that start with jump-ins can be broken. If you’re close, break using throw, but watch out... your opponent can counter your throw attempt with an invincible move or super.
