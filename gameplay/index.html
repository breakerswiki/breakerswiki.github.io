• Controls

    Breakers Revenge is commonly played using the square layout A C B D with light and heavy punches (A and C) on top, then light and heavy kicks (B and D) on the bottom.

  • Throw

    Input forward or back + C.
    Some characters have extra throws inputs. They're also great for breaking, interrupting specific jumps and combos with low risk.

  • Supers

    The Super Meter holds up to 3 stocks, each used for powerful moves with specific inputs. Build meter by using attacks, dashes, taunts, or taking and blocking hits.

  • Juggles

    Extend combos by juggling opponents with specials, supers or jumps. Each character has unique juggle properties, adding variety and depth.

  • Back roll

    Press 3 buttons when knocked down, it helps escape pressure after a non-super hit, giving you a better chance to reset the situation and avoid further attacks.

  • Dashes

    Input forward or back twice.
    All except Condor can dash. Some gain invincibility on backdash, others can cancel dashes for movement tricks or longer jumps.

  • Dizzy

    Repeated hits can cause dizziness. While dizzy, mashing directions and buttons skips the effect, triggering immediate recovery with brief invincibility frames.

  • Chip damage

    Chip damage occurs while blocking and can secure a win when the opponent's health is low. Supers deal minimal chip damage, while specials are more effective for finishing off an opponent.

  • Break

    Interrupt certain combos with a reversal attack. Focus on mastering unbreakable combos, identifying breakable ones, and strategically using them to bait reversals.

  • Risk system

    Increases damage over time if you're inactive. Winners take more damage; losers gain slight defense. The player with lower health is immune to damage boosts until both players health evens out.
