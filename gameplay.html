Button layout

Light punch Heavy punch
Light kick Heavy Kick

Throw

Left or Right +

Throws Beat Meaty Normals. Some Characters have Extra Throw Inputs or Air Throws. Throwing is also an effective way to use the break mechanic.

Supers

This gauge holds 3 stocks, each full stock allows a Super move.

Build meter by performing normals, specials, dashes, or taunts. Also by getting hit or blocking.

Tech rolling

On wake-up, press 3 buttons. ++

Tech roll give you more options to escape from your opponent's pressure after a knockdown. It only works after after a non-super hit. Tech rolls can also be delayed

Dashes

Left x2 or Right x2

All characters except Condor can dash. Some backdashes are invincible. Certain characters can cancel dashes to quickly adjust distances or jumps.

Dizzy

To recover from a dizzy, mash buttons and directions.

Repeated hits can cause dizziness, but mashing directions and buttons skips it, triggering immediate recovery with brief invincibility frames.

Chip damage

Chip damage happens during block and can secure a win when the opponent's health is low. Super moves deal little chip damage, special moves are more effective for finishing off an opponent.

Breakers

The green circle showing a successful Breaker, similar to wakeup reversals.

A Breaker is a defensive tool that lets you escape specific combos. It can be triggered by specials, normals, backdashes, throws, or supers.

Master unbreakable combos for guaranteed damage and identify your opponent’s breakable combos. Use it for mind games, like baiting defenders into reversals.

Risk system

Watch the damage increase over time.

The Risk System increases the damage of your next attack over time only when you're not attacking or defending.

Damage caps vary by character. After about 30s in-game, the next hit can deal double damage. The round winner has reduced damage resistance in the next round, while the loser receives a slight defense boost.

When players have significantly different health, the one with less health is immune to damage modification until their health levels are similar.
