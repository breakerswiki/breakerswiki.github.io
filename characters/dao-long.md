## Guide

### Overview
Dao-Long is a well-rounded character with fast charge inputs and a quick dash, making it easy to switch between zoning and offense. His Super “Shin Kyoku Ranbu” deals high-damage and combos from most normals and air juggles.


<div class="grid">
<div class="card"><video>
  <source data-src="media/characters/dao-long/videos/dao-long_offense.mp4"">
</video>

#### Attack
* **Projectile ([1]6P)**: Builds meter and applies chip damage; recovery is fast enough to bait and punish jump-in attempts at specific ranges.
* **Palm (A+C)**:  Safe on block; Serves as a reliable hit-confirm into specials and Supers.
</div>


<div class="card"><video>
   <source data-src="media/characters/dao-long/videos/dao-long_defense.mp4"">
 </video>

#### Defense
* **Senkyuu Tai ([1]8K)**:  Quick close-range anti-air; The light version, enables a Super juggle for if it connects at the peak of the opponent's jump.
* **Close Normals**:  close 5C/5D are super-cancelable on hit for high-damage conversions.
* **4+C**: works in close range as a powerful OS, triggering an anti‑air or a throw.
</div>



<div class="card"><video>
  <source data-src="media/characters/dao-long/videos/dao-long_supers.mp4"">
</video>
          
#### Super moves
* **Houshin Renpa ([4]646P)**: Invincible startup, projectile passthrough, reliable A+C confirm.
* **Shin Kyoku Ranbu ([4]646K)**: Main Super for air juggles and easy confirms from normals or A+C.
* **Renkyuu Tai ([1]319K)**: Invincible vertical anti-air; mash kick buttons to increase the number of hits.
</div>



<div class="card"><video>
    <source data-src="media/characters/dao-long/videos/dao-long_dashes.mp4"">

#### Dashes
* **Forward Dash** : Fast recovery, Ideal for pressure (light blockstring &gt; dash &gt; throw) <br> You can follow with a Super: [1]6.6.4.6K
* **Backdash** :  Invulnerable startup but with slow recovery.
</div>



<div class="card"><video>
  <source data-src="media/characters/dao-long/videos/fuzzy.mp4"">
</video>

#### Fuzzy / Instant overhead
* Perform a fuzzy jump with jump C > neutral jump B. 
* Neutral jump B is also an instant overhead (though it doesn't work on all characters).
</div>


<div class="card"><video>
  <source data-src="media/characters/dao-long/videos/throwOS.mp4"">

#### Throw Option select
* Coupling Dash with Back Throw creates an aggressive throw/anti‑air OS, especially effective against opponents trying to escape by jumping
