Overview

Maherl is a slow, poke-focused character with safe long-range specials. He lacks super or special cancels from low attacks and relies on gimmicks like Abodement Step and Super Genie, making him challenging to play. However, his long-range options can catch opponents off guard when timed well.

Swords

214P
214P can be hit confirmed from 5C, 2C, 5D, and 5B.

214K
214K works great as an anti-air and juggle confirm up close after 5D or 5C.

Supers

Cadabra Bomber

Maherl summons a genie that delivers a powerful punch. This super is safe on block and has invincible startup frames.

⚠️ There's a gap right before the genie throws his punch. On block, some characters can use their invincible moves to dodge the punch and punish.

Shiva Dance

A series of sword strikes that can go through projectiles when used up close. This super is also often used after canceling a normal anti-air move like 5B.

Rolling Press

A throw that sends the opponent to the other side of the screen, often used after a whiffed Abodement Step, during a Break, or with the Sword Glitch.

Step/Sword glitch

A+C > Kara 214P or 214K

1 frame cancel window to get the glitch :

To trigger the glitch, you need to cancel A+C with a sword special at the 3rd frame.

  • ✅ Frame 3 of A+C = sword glitch
  • ❌ Frame 2 or sooner = sword
  • ❌ Frame 4 or later = A+C
Sword glitch variations

HP/HK swords:
Maherl travels to the opposite side of the opponent.

LP/LK swords:
Result in a shorter distance, Maherl stays on the same side.

Body splash:
During the peak of flight, Maherl can perform a 2C (body splash) that stays active throughout the entire descent.

If you don't do anything during the glitch, Maherl teleports back to the ground. Seize the opportunity to surprise your opponent with a throw or 360 super.
