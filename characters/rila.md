## Guide

### Overview
Rila is a charge character with high damage output. She has no zoning tool except for her down heavy punch, but her fast anti-air and anti-projectile super help her control space and shut down opponents from anywhere on the screen.


### Offense
<video>
  <source data-src="media/characters/rila/videos/rila_attack.mp4">
</video>

* 2C: Difficult for opponents to contest. Safe on block. Works well as an anti-air.
* Neutral Jump C: Has priority over most normals and also anti-air specials outside their invincibility frames.
* Quick Scratch ([1]6A): Fast approach tool. Safe except against Tia and Saizo.




### Defense
<video>
  <source data-src="media/characters/rila/videos/rila_defense.mp4">
</video>

* [1]8K: Long reach anti-air. Can miss up close, especially against cross-ups.
* [1]6C: [1].6C hits airborne and connects into Super ([1]319K). It deals more damage than the light version but is unsafe on block.
* [1]3D: Pass through projectiles. Has a super follow-up ([1]319K) ; On block, cancel into Super to bait and catch punishes.
* Anti-air normals:
  * close 5C
  * close 5D against crossups
  * 5A can convert into [1]6C and [1]319K.


### Super moves
<video>
  <source data-src="media/characters/rila/videos/anti-air.mp4">
</video>

* Bloody Fang ([1]319K): Punishes projectiles and extends combos from [1].3D or anti-air [1].6C.
* Shine Beast ([1]6 4 6P): Has invincible startup; hold and release at any moment. If you hold it too long, Rila goes back to neutral.
