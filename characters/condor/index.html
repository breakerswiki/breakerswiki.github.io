Overview

Condor is a grappler with powerful throws, but his limited mobility and lack of defensive options make him vulnerable to zoning characters.

Attack

Sky Bridge (236K): Your main meterless hit confirm.

Grand Buffalo Horn (66K): Goes through projectiles and helps close distance, but it's punishable on whiff or block. Use it safely after a knockdown.

Neck Hanging Tree (3A/B): A multi-hit command throw that launches the opponent, allowing a follow-up super (236236P).

Jumps: Use Heel Stomp (Air ↓+D) as your main jump-in. it's fast and stays active. Body Press (Air ↓+C) is also great for crossups; mix with jB to stay in front.

Defense

Tomahawk (A+B+C) : Acts as an anti-air and stops most projectiles (except Saizo’s kunai). Can be canceled from certain normals for a super juggle. Use it carefully in neutral, as some characters (Saizo, Dao-Long, Tia, Rila, Sho) can punish on whiff or is block.

5A and close 5C: Super and special cancelable. Great for countering close jump-Ins. Cancel with Tomahawk, then follow with a super juggle

Supers

Atlas Rock Buster (236236P): It has invincibility, making it useful as a wakeup reversal or to counter close projectiles. It can also be confirmed from light attacks.

Moonlight Shake (720P/K): Grabs at close range and deals significant damage.
