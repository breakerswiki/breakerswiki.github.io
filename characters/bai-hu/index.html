Bai-Hu, the final boss of Breakers Revenge, initiated the tournament to discover the strongest fighters.

How to unlock Bai-Hu: At the character select screen, rotate P1’s joystick counterclockwise and P2’s joystick clockwise.

⚠️ Bai-Hu’s overpowered moves give him an unfair advantage, leading to his ban in competitive play.
