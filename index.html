DISCOVER THE BREAKERS GAMES

  • Breakers

    1996
    Neo Geo MVS/AES/CD, Dreamcast
    Visco Corporation

    History began with the prototype Crystal Legacy, which later evolve into Breakers, a fast-paced and accessible fighting game built around intuitive and responsive controls. Its visual identity drew inspiration from established fighting game franchises of the era. Following its arcade debut on the Neo Geo MVS, the game was released on the Neo Geo AES and Neo Geo CD, with later the legacy port on Dreamcast bringing it to a wider audience.

  • Breakers Revenge

    1998
    Neo Geo MVS
    Visco Corporation

    Breakers Revenge is building upon the original Breakers with roster balance adjustments, new stage designs, additional color palettes, and the introduction of a new character, the ninja Saizo. Today, Breakers Revenge remains the most actively played entry thanks to a dedicated online community and enduring competitive scene, making it the standard version for tournaments and online play,

  • Breakers Collection

    2023
    Xbox Series, PC, PS4/5, Switch
    QUByte Interactive

    Breakers Collection is a modern compilation that brings together both Breakers and Breakers Revenge in a single package. Designed for modern platforms, it features rollback netcode, cross-platform play, ranked matchmaking, global leaderboards, and an interactive training mode. The collection also includes a dedicated art gallery, Team Battle mode, and various quality-of-life improvements

  • Breakers Revenge Chicago

    2024
    exA System
    exA-Arcadia

    Breakers Revenge Chicago is an expanded version of Breakers Revenge with updated visuals, a remastered soundtrack, an enhanced training mode, and the addition of Bai-Hu as a playable character. This exclusive exA-Arcadia release includes three distinct game modes: Revenge, which recreates the original arcade version; Extra, based on the Neo-Geo CD release and Chicago, a rebalanced mode featuring gameplay adjustments such as short hops and additional tweaks.

How to play

Learn the mechanics

Controls

Breakers is generally played on a square 4 button layout.

C
D
Heavy punch
Heavy kick

Dashes

or

All characters (except Condor) can dash by tapping forward or back twice. Some characters have backdash invincibility and dash cancels.

Throw

( or ) + C

Throws are strong due to the absence of throw teching. At close range, it is also a great defensive option, allowing you to use them to break combos that start with a close jump-in.

Combos

Breakers features generous input buffers and versatile Super juggles. Every action builds meter, and each Super consumes 1 bar.

Back roll

A+B+C

After a non-super hit, press 3 buttons while knocked down to back-roll and escape okizeme setups.

Dizzy

Taking repeated hits causes dizziness. Mash directional inputs and buttons to recover faster. Recovery grants brief invincibility.

Chip damage

Blocking special moves inflicts chip damage which can KO. Supers deal minimal chip damage.

Break

When getting hit, interrupt certain combos with a reversal. Use breakable combos for extra damage on unaware opponents and unbreakable combos for guaranteed damage. See the character guides for specific examples.

Risk system

Comeback mechanics in Breakers:

Damage + Defense boost on low life
The player with more health takes increased damage until the life bars equalize.

Damage boost by waiting
Up to double damage on your next hit.

Match winner penalty
The winner takes more damage, while the loser gains defense. The effect stacks with each win.

Additional modes

Chicago and Extra modes

Chicago Mode

Featured in Breakers Revenge Chicago. Adds short hops, balance adjustments, and selectable close/far normals independent of spacing.

Extra Mode

Available in Neo-Geo CD and Chicago releases. Increases combo fluidity, cancels, escape options, and defensive mechanics.

How to access and use training mode

Getting Started

Requires a Fightcade setup with the Breakers Revenge ROM installed.

  1. Fightcade: Join the Breakers Revenge channel and select TRAINING.
  2. Standalone: Run fcadefbneo.exe, then navigate to Game › Lua Scripting › Fightcade Training Mode.

Record & Playback options

  1. Start Recording: Press Coin three times (a red indicator appears).
  2. Record Inputs: Perform the inputs you want to record (defaults to P2).
  3. Stop Recording: Press Coin once.
  4. Play Back: Press Coin again to trigger playback.
