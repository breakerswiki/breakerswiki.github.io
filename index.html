📖 History

1982 - VISCO Corporation

Founded in 1982, VISCO Corporation, the creator of the Breakers series, is a Japanese video game developer known for its arcade and console titles. Initially part of Taito, VISCO collaborated with Seta and Sammy on SSV and, since 2008, has focused on slot machines for Southeast Asia.

1996 - Breakers

Breakers, released on March 21, 1996, for the Neo Geo MVS, is a fighting game developed by VISCO and published by SNK. Known for its fluid combat and unique characters, it gained a cult following over time. Breakers was initially announced in 1993 under the title Tenrin no Syo Chicago and was referred to as Crystal Legacy outside Japan.

1998 - Breakers Revenge

Released on July 2, 1998, for the Neo Geo MVS and AES by the same companies, Breakers Revenge updates the previous game by offering new colors, reworked stages, and introducing a new character, the ninja Saizo.

2023 - Breakers Collection

On January 12, 2023, Breakers Collection, a re-release for consoles and PC by QUByte Interactive was launched, including Breakers and Breakers Revenge, and featuring cross-platform online play, leaderboards, new artwork, a training mode, and team versus.

2024 - Breakers Revenge Chicago

Breakers Revenge Chicago, released on August 22, 2024, is an arcade update for the EXA-Arcadia system. This version features Bai-Hu as a playable character, additional colors, a training mode, classic stages, the Neo Geo CD soundtrack arranged by Kenichi Kamio, a new announcer, and three gameplay modes: Revenge (original), Extra (Neo Geo CD secret mode), and Chicago (rebalanced gameplay with short hop ability).
