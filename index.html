Breakers

1996

Neo Geo MVS/AES/CD
Visco Corporation
VISCO was founded in 1993, with the prototype Crystal Legacy, later evolving into Breakers, a fast, accessible fighting game with intuitive inputs and quick movement. Console releases followed on Neo Geo AES, Neo Geo CD, and Dreamcast.

Controls

Breakers is generally played on a square 4 button layout.

Dashes

or

All characters (except Condor) can dash by tapping forward or back twice. Some characters have backdash invincibility and dash cancels.

Throw

( or ) + C

Throws are powerful due to the absence of throw teching. They can also interrupt close jump-ins.

Combos

Breakers features generous input buffers and versatile Super juggles. Every action builds meter, and each Super consumes 1 bar.

Back roll

A+B+C

After a non-super hit, press 3 buttons while knocked down to back-roll and escape okizeme setups.

Dizzy

Taking repeated hits causes dizziness. Mash directional inputs and buttons to recover faster. Recovery grants brief invincibility.

Chip damage

Blocking special moves inflicts chip damage which can KO. Supers deal minimal chip damage.

Break

Interrupt certain combos with a reversal throw or special. Use breakable combos for damage and unbreakable combos for safer pressure.

Risk system

Comeback mechanics in Breakers:

Damage + Defense boost on low life
The player with more health takes increased damage until the life bars equalize.

Damage boost by waiting
Up to double damage on your next hit.

Match winner penalty
The winner takes more damage, while the loser gains defense. The effect stacks with each win.

Chicago and Extra modes

Chicago Mode

Featured in Breakers Revenge Chicago. Adds short hops, balance adjustments, and selectable close/far normals independent of spacing.

Extra Mode

Available in Neo-Geo CD and Chicago releases. Increases combo fluidity, cancels, escape options, and defensive mechanics.

How to access and use training mode

Getting Started

Requires a Fightcade setup with the Breakers Revenge ROM installed.

  1. Fightcade: Join the Breakers Revenge channel and select TRAINING.
  2. Standalone: Run fcadefbneo.exe, then navigate to Game › Lua Scripting › Fightcade Training Mode.

Record & Playback options

  1. Start Recording: Press Coin three times (a red indicator appears).
  2. Record Inputs: Perform the inputs you want to record (defaults to P2).
  3. Stop Recording: Press Coin once.
  4. Play Back: Press Coin again to trigger playback.
