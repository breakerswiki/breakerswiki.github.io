Discover the Breakers games
Breakers

1996

Neo Geo MVS
Visco Corporation
VISCO began in 1993 with the prototype Crystal Legacy, which later evolved into Breakers, a fast, accessible fighting game built around simple inputs and quick movement. Console versions followed later on Neo Geo AES, Neo Geo CD, and eventually Dreamcast.
Breakers Revenge

1998

Neo Geo MVS
Visco Corporation
The most popular entry in the series. This enhanced sequel updates the original with new character artwork, balance adjustments, additional color variations, and redesigned stages. It also introduces a new character, the ninja Saizo.
Breakers Collection

2023

Xbox Series, PC, PS4/5, Switch
QUByte Interactive
A compilation of both Breakers games, featuring modern additions such as rollback netcode with cross‑platform play, ranked matches, global leaderboards, an expanded training mode, an art gallery, and a new Team Battle mode.
Breakers Revenge Chicago

2024

exA System
exA-Arcadia
This upgraded EXA‑Arcadia release features new artwork, colors, training mode, new soundtrack, and all stage versions. Bai‑Hu is playable, and the game offers 3 modes: Revenge (Classic), Extra (Neo‑Geo CD), and Chicago (short hops and balance changes).

Play and join the community

Official releases

Originally released on Neo Geo MVS, Breakers and its sequel are also available on modern platforms through Breakers Collection (2023), which adds rollback netcode, cross-platform play support, training, and a team battle mode.

Play online

Arcade versions remain accessible through emulation, with Fightcade being the most popular way to play Breakers today. It adds features such as fast online matchmaking, match replays, spectator modes, and dedicated training tools for competitive play.

Breakers community

The Breakers Discord serves as the main hub for finding players, exploring advanced techniques, and staying updated on community events, including offline tournaments where the game is often played on original arcade cabinets.

How to play

Everything you need to know

Controls

Breakers is generally played on a square 4 button layout.

Dashes

or
All characters (except for Condor) can dash by tapping forward or back twice. Some characters have backdash invincibility and dash‑cancels, giving them enhanced mobility options.

Throw

( or ) + C
Throw is a strong option in Breakers due to the absence of throw‑teching. Throws can also break close jump‑in combos. Certain characters have additional throw inputs or air throws.

Combos

Breakers offers numerous cancel options, a generous input buffer, and multiple ways to use Super, including anti‑air and air‑to‑air juggles. Every action builds meter, with each Super consuming a single meter.

Back roll

A+B+C
After a non-super hit, press 3 buttons while knocked down to do a back-roll. This can help you defend against strong okizeme setups.

Dizzy

Taking repeated hits will leave you dizzy. While stunned, mash directions and buttons to recover faster. The recovery animation grants a brief window of invincibility.

Chip damage

Blocking inflicts chip damage, which can lead to a KO. Use special moves to secure chip finishes, as Supers deal very little chip damage.

Break

Break lets you interrupt certain combos with a throw or the appropriate move. Use breakable combos for extra damage when the opponent isn’t ready, and unbreakable combos when you want a safer option.

Risk system

Comeback Mechanics

Damage + Defense boost on low life
The healthier player takes more damage until both life bars are equal.

Damage boost by waiting
Up to double damage on your next hit.

Match winner takes more damage, loser gains defense
These effects stack with each win. In competitive play the game is often reset to keep things fair.



Bonus

Additional modes

Chicago and Extra modes

Chicago Mode

Available in Breakers Revenge Chicago, this mode adds balance changes, short hops, and allows characters to choose close or far normals independently of spacing through a new move list.

Extra Mode

Extra Mode makes Breakers considerably more explosive by giving players many more ways to cancel, extend combos, escape pressure, and survive situations that would normally lead to a KO. This mode is available in the Neo‑Geo CD release of Breakers and in Breakers Revenge Chicago.

How to access and use training mode

Getting Started

You need a Fightcade setup. Install the client, create an account, and place the Breakers Revenge ROM in your ROMs folder.

  1. Fightcade: Join the Breakers Revenge room and select TRAINING.
  2. Standalone: Launch fcadefbneo.exe, then open Game › Lua Scripting › Fightcade Training Mode.

Record & Playback options

  1. Press Coin 3 times. A red indicator will appear in the top-left corner.
  2. Perform the inputs you want to record (Player 2 by default), then press Coin to stop.
  3. Press Coin again to play back the recording.
