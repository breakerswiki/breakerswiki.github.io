Introduction

Breakers Revenge, released in 1998, is a fighting game developed by Visco and published by SNK. It's the sequel to "Breakers" which came out a year earlier.

Breaker’s Revenge features a roster of unique fighters with distinct styles and moves. Though less famous than other titles, it remains a favorite among retro gamers and Neo Geo fans.
