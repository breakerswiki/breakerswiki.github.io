📖 History

1982 - VISCO Corporation

Founded in 1982, VISCO Corporation, the creator of the Breakers series, is a Japanese video game developer known for its arcade and NES titles, later expanding to Neo Geo and Nintendo 64. Originally part of Taito, VISCO released some games under that label and collaborated with Seta and Sammy on SSV arcade titles. Since 2008, the company has focused on slot machines for Southeast Asian casinos.

1998 - Breakers Revenge

Released in 1998 on Neo-Geo systems, Breakers Revenge is the sequel to Breaker's which came out a year earlier. It's a fighting game developed by VISCO and published by SNK.

2023 - Breakers Collection

Twenty-five years later comes Breakers Collection, a re-release for consoles and PC by QUByte Interactive. It includes Breakers and Breakers Revenge and features cross-platform online play, leaderboards, new artwork, a training mode, and team versus.

2024 - Breakers Revenge Chicago

Developed by VISCO and EXA-Arcadia for their arcade system, this version offers new features, additional colors, training mode, classic stages, the Neo Geo CD soundtrack with an arranged version by Kenichi Kamio, a new announcer, and 3 gameplay modes: Revenge (original gameplay), Extra (Neo Geo CD secret mode), and Chicago (rebalanced with short hop)
